> *"Ruleset" for the Hackathon Minigame "AI GATE*

In this miniproject the aim is to prove that you *are* a robot.
It sounds weird (because it's usually the opposite), but that was the topic for the Hackathon “Functionally Dysfunctional”.

There were three winners, sadly not me though.  
A stream on twitch took place, so the host (Lewis) could take a look into the submissions. He got to my project, and very close to the ending, but ultimately failed to beat it!! :d

Can *you* beat it though?
You can try [here](https://heliacer.github.io/aigate/#)

No hints.  
Prior knowledge in browser inspection is kinda needed.  
Good luck lol.

All the results can be found on the [Hackathon website](https://hackathon.lewismenelaws.com/#)

If you want to see all the other submissions including mine, you can watch the highlights in this hackathon's [youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1hgN3mwE7jc)
